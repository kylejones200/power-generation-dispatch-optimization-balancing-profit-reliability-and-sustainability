During California's August 2020 heat wave, utilities with optimized dispatch algorithms seamlessly balanced coal baseload, natural gas peaking, wind variability, and solar intermittency—keeping lights on while minimizing fuel costs. Those relying on manual dispatch experienced both higher costs and near-miss reliability events.

This tutorial covers generation dispatch optimization: merit order algorithms, unit commitment with start/stop costs, renewable integration with uncertainty, and emissions-constrained dispatch. The code demonstrates how to minimize fuel costs while maintaining reliability and environmental compliance across diverse generation portfolios.

This matters because a 1% improvement in dispatch efficiency translates to millions of dollars annually for large utilities. The challenge involves economic dispatch, unit commitment, reserve requirements, transmission constraints, environmental compliance, and ramping capabilities—all optimized in real-time as conditions evolve.
